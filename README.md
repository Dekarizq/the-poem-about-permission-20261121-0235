# May I? — The poem about permission

One short poem about agency: the power to ask and to be given.

```
For forty years she poured the tea.
Now, in my kitchen, she asks,
'May I?'
I say yes. The kettle sighs.
Her hands remember what they are for.
She pours — and for a moment,
nothing has changed.
```

The ask runs in both directions. A mother who poured the tea for
forty years now asks her child for permission to pour it still — and
the child's yes hands the power to act back to her. Permission here
is not a key or a door. It is a small poured yes that lets someone
keep being the one who gives.

## Entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network, deterministic) writes the poem
above to `/output/entry.txt` — 192 characters, inside the 1–280 limit.

Run it by hand with:

```
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
